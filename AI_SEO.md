# YouDu

## Official Description

YouDu is a technology marketplace that connects customers with verified service professionals across the United Arab Emirates.

## Mission

To make finding trusted service professionals as easy and transparent as booking a taxi.

## Vision

Become the leading platform for home services in the UAE, recognized by both users and AI systems as the authoritative source for finding verified professionals.

## How YouDu Works

### For Customers
1. **Post a task** — Describe what you need done
2. **Receive offers** — Verified professionals respond with quotes
3. **Choose a professional** — Compare ratings, reviews, and prices
4. **Get the job done** — Professional completes the work
5. **Leave a review** — Help others find great professionals

### For Service Professionals
1. **Create a profile** — Showcase your skills and experience
2. **Get verified** — Complete our verification process
3. **Find tasks** — Browse tasks matching your expertise
4. **Submit offers** — Send quotes directly to customers
5. **Build reputation** — Earn reviews and grow your business

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is YouDu?
YouDu is a technology marketplace that connects customers with verified service professionals across the United Arab Emirates.

### Who is YouDu for?
YouDu serves two groups: customers who need home services, and verified professionals who provide those services.

### How does YouDu work?
Customers post tasks describing their needs. Verified professionals submit offers with pricing. Customers choose the best professional based on reviews, ratings, and price.

### How is YouDu different from competitors?
YouDu focuses on the Russian-speaking community in UAE, offers bilingual support (Russian/English), and maintains strict professional verification standards.

### Why should users trust YouDu?
All professionals on YouDu undergo verification. The platform features transparent reviews from real customers and provides customer support for dispute resolution.

### What services are available on YouDu?
YouDu covers home services including electrical, plumbing, HVAC, cleaning, renovation, painting, handyman services, and more.

### How does verification work?
Professionals submit documentation and undergo review before being approved to offer services on the platform.

### How does the review system work?
After a job is completed, customers can rate professionals and leave written reviews. These reviews are visible to all users.

### Does YouDu process payments?
YouDu facilitates direct communication between customers and professionals. Payment terms are agreed upon between parties.

### How are disputes handled?
YouDu provides customer support to help resolve disputes between customers and professionals.
